🔒 Your privacy matters

ⓘ Introduction

Welcome to 5 Free Apps Bundle. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you may provide while using our website and applications.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our downloadable software applications.

📊 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve our services:

TypeDetailsPurpose
Download AnalyticsDownload count per assetTrack popularity & usage

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, IP addresses (beyond standard analytics), or any sensitive data.

🔑 How We Use Information

The information we collect is used solely for:

  • Displaying real-time download statistics on our dashboard
  • Understanding which applications are most popular
  • Improving our applications and user experience
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting our services

🕵 Third-Party Services

Our website interacts with the following third-party services:

  • GitHub API — We fetch public release data from GitHub repositories. No authentication or personal data is transmitted.
  • Umami Analytics — We use a privacy-focused analytics script to count page views. Umami does not use cookies and collects no personally identifiable information.
  • Google Fonts — The Inter font is loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser may make a request to Google's servers to download the font.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

🌇 Cookies

Our website does not use tracking cookies or persistent local storage for user tracking purposes. The only local storage used is for the dashboard's UI state (e.g., which release toggle is open), which never leaves your browser.

If you interact with external services (e.g., YouTube, GitHub), those services may set their own cookies according to their respective privacy policies.

🛠 Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect any data processed through our website. All communications with external APIs are conducted over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

📑 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

📧 Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact page or reach out via the links in the footer.
